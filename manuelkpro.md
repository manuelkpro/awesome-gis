| Aspect               | Traditional HCI                                     | Active Human-AI Interaction (AHI)        |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Interface-Based**  | Uses keyboards, touchscreens, and GUIs.           | AI interprets human intent and context dynamically. |
| **User-Controlled**  | User initiates actions; system responds.           | AI collaborates and learns from interactions. |
| **Static Design**    | Fixed functionalities, requiring manual updates.  | AI continuously evolves through machine learning. |
| **Limited Adaptability** | Follows predefined rules, lacks flexibility.      | AI tailors interactions based on user behavior. |
| **Task-Oriented**   | Focuses on efficiency for specific tasks.         | AI proactively assists and anticipates user needs. |
